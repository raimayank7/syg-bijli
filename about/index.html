About Bijli Bill

Bijli Bill helps households understand and estimate their electricity bill: a bill calculator that works slab by slab, an appliance units calculator, a meter reading calculator, and plain guides to reading a bill, slab rates, fixed charges and using less electricity.

Tariffs: only from official orders, with dates

Electricity tariffs are fixed state by state by each state's electricity regulatory commission and revised often. We print a tariff figure only when we have read it in the regulator's order or the distribution company's own document, and we give its source and the date we read it. For every other supplier, the calculator takes the rates you type from your own bill, and no number is printed.

  • Punjab (PSPCL), domestic supply, FY 2026-27 (valid 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027): PSPCL, "Existing Tariff and the new tariff for FY 2026-27" (Annexure-A), published by Punjab State Power Corporation Limited at docs.pspcl.in, reproducing the tariff determined in the PSERC Tariff Order for FY 2026-27 for PSPCL, issued on 6 March 2026 (Petition No. 70 of 2025). Read on 12 November 2026. The site uses only the domestic rows of that table and its two footnotes (fixed charge on 80% of sanctioned load; fuel adjustment, voltage and time-of-day provisions apply in addition).

That table does not give electricity duty, cesses, meter rent, any state subsidy or the billing cycle, so we do not state them. No other state's tariff is on this site, because we could not open and read an official current schedule for it when the site was written. If a tariff here is out of date, please tell us.

Appliance wattages

The wattage chart and the list in the units calculator give typical ratings of common household models, written from general knowledge. They are a starting point only. The rating plate, the manual or the star label of your own appliance gives its real figure, and appliances that cycle on and off use less than rating × hours.

Guides

All guides are our own writing. They explain how bills, slabs, loads, meters and labels work in general, and deliberately leave out figures that differ by state or change often (duties, subsidies, penalties, fees, scheme amounts), pointing instead to the supplier, the regulator or the official portal concerned. Written in November 2026.

Nothing is stored

The calculators run in your browser. Rates and appliances you enter are remembered only in your own browser so that you need not retype them.

What this site is not

It is an independent information site. It is not an electricity supplier or a government site, cannot take payments or complaints, and is not connected with any distribution company, regulator or appliance maker named on it. Electrical work is dangerous: have wiring, earthing and meters handled only by a licensed electrician or the supplier.
